TZ Glasses HUD

What the captain sees through the Ray-Ban Display, and the TZ Glasses app on the iPhone. The background is the real world; the glasses only add the bright labels. Drag the view or use the arrow keys to turn your head.

Sim time 00:00

The dashed square is the display in the right lens, about 20° wide. It's see-through: black adds nothing, so only the labels light up over the water. Everything outside the square is just the real world. In daylight the world looks a little darker because the lenses tint outdoors, which is what keeps the display readable in sun.

What you're looking at

Night lens dims everything except marks, which glow, and alarms, which stay bright, so gear stands out without dazzling. Night red does the same in red, for night vision. With Switch at sunset on, the lens stays on Day in daylight and changes once the sun is down (Dusk or Night above). Picture mode only.

Picture mode: the app draws the lens as an image. Full colour and exact placement.

Activating is the same as starting navigation on the route in TimeZero. The active legs turn into a glowing rainbow road on the water, flowing toward the next waypoint.

Look ahead for the route, the lobster pots and the shoal; look astern for your own track. "Joe's spot" dead ahead carries TimeZero's skull symbol, so it counts as a danger whatever it's called: inside 0.5 nm it turns red and sounds the alarm.
